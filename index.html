Unstitch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Unstitch is a point-of-sale and inventory app for unstitched-fabric retail shops. It is operated by ZevGeeks ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data the app collects

  • Product photos — photographs of fabric taken by shop staff to display inventory. These are pictures of products, not people.
  • Customer name and phone number — recorded optionally at the time of a sale, only when a customer chooses to share them with the shop.
  • Shop account email address — used solely to sign the shop's own account in to its synchronisation backend. Sign-in is optional; the app functions without it.
  • Sales records — the shop's own business data (items sold, prices, dates).

How data is stored and transmitted

All data is stored on the shop's device first. When a shop connects its account, data is synchronised over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS) to a private database hosted on Supabase, accessible only to that shop's authenticated account.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not show advertising and do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not collect location data, contacts, messages, or device identifiers for tracking.

Data deletion

To request deletion of data held about you — for example, a name or phone number you gave a shop at the till — contact us at the address below and we will remove it from the synchronisation backend. You may also ask the shop directly.

Children

The app is a business tool intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.
